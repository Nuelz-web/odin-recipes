Omelette

Description

A quick and fluffy omelette perfect for breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tablespoon milk
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tablespoon oil or butter

Instructions

  1. Beat eggs with milk, salt and pepper.
  2. Heat oil in a pan.
  3. Pour the egg mix and cook.
  4. Fold and serve hot.
